As a Research Software Engineer at the Knight Cancer Institute, Oregon Health \& Science University, I build the infrastructure and tooling that let cancer researchers move data and analysis from raw sequencing output to publishable results. \\
My work spans the stack: I lead development of a workflow orchestration system that runs genomic pipelines across cloud and HPC environments, build and operate the cloud infrastructure and CI/CD systems those pipelines depend on, and, more recently, deploy and fine-tune large language models to prototype AI-assisted developer and research workflows. \\
That range is what I bring to a new team: the ability to pick up an unfamiliar stack quickly, build tools that non-technical users actually trust, and translate between engineers and the researchers or stakeholders who depend on their work.
