To Whom It May Concern,

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

I currently work at OHSU as a Research Software Engineer at the Knight Cancer Institute, where I build the data infrastructure and system-to-system interfaces that keep genomic and clinical research pipelines running. I'm writing to apply for the System Application Analyst position on the HPSA team --- drawn to the chance to bring that same interface and infrastructure work directly into clinical operations, and to do it inside an institution I already know well.

\paragraph{EXPERIENCE}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

Much of what this role asks for --- system-to-system interface design, technical application support, incident visibility, documentation, and managing relationships across technical and non-technical stakeholders --- overlaps directly with what I already do day to day:

\begin{itemize}

\item Designed and built data-interoperability interfaces on healthcare data standards, including an HL7 FHIR client (\link{https://fhir-aggregator.org/}{FHIR-Aggregator}) integrating with Google's Cloud Healthcare API to unify clinical and genomic data, and implementations of the GA4GH Data Repository Service (DRS) and Task Execution API (TES) standards --- direct experience specifying and building system-to-system interfaces in a healthcare data context.

\item Provide ongoing technical application support for the Knight Cancer Institute's research data platform, including troubleshooting, upgrades, and database administration (Postgres/Elasticsearch), plus real-time status monitoring and incident visibility for production services.

\item Coordinate technical work and delivery milestones with external \link{https://www.ga4gh.org/}{GA4GH} consortium partners, and regularly translate system behavior into plain language for non-technical researchers and clinicians --- the kind of stakeholder management this role requires at OHSU's scale.

\item Built and maintained cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, OpenStack), including service provisioning, backup/restore, and operational reliability --- hands-on exposure to the security review, patching, and operational maintenance this role's HPSA responsibilities call for.

\end{itemize}

\paragraph{WHY THIS ROLE}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

I'll be upfront about where my background lines up less directly: I haven't supported CVIS, Echo, Cath Lab, or GI-specific clinical systems before, and I don't have direct EPIC or medical-device-integration experience. What I do bring is real, adjacent experience --- healthcare data standards, system interface design, and application support --- plus two-plus years of already navigating OHSU's IT environment, security posture, and ways of working, which should shorten the runway most external candidates would need.

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

I'm drawn to this role specifically because it moves my work closer to patient care: the cardiovascular and procedural systems this team supports are further downstream, more directly tied to clinicians and outcomes, than the research pipelines I build today, and that's a shift I want to make. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background could translate to this role. Please see my attached resume for further detail.
