\paragraph{EXPERIENCE}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

Across my work at the OHSU Knight Cancer Institute, I have taken on responsibilities that reach well beyond my title:

\begin{itemize}

\item Architected and lead development of \link{https://github.com/calypr/funnel}{Funnel}, a distributed task execution toolkit implementing the \link{https://www.ga4gh.org/task-execution-services/}{GA4GH TES API} --- a system that schedules, dispatches, and monitors compute workloads across heterogeneous cloud and HPC backends, with explicit attention to resource locality, scheduling overhead, and cross-platform portability

\item Co-authored \link{https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10564576}{``The GA4GH Task Execution Application Programming Interface: Enabling Easy Multicloud Task Execution''} (IEEE Computing in Science \& Engineering, 2024), a peer-reviewed publication describing the design and real-world deployment of a multi-cloud workload execution standard --- the kind of systems-level writing Intel cites as a preferred qualification

\item Built and maintained cloud-native infrastructure across AWS, Azure, GCP, and OpenStack using Kubernetes, Helm, and Terraform, including automated provisioning, backup/restore systems, and compliance workflows for genomic data platforms

\item Contributed to \link{https://github.com/nextflow-io/nf-ga4gh}{nf-ga4gh} (Nextflow's GA4GH plugin), \link{https://github.com/uc-cdis/gen3-workflow}{Gen3-Workflow}, and \link{https://github.com/anvilproject/drs_downloader}{DRS Downloader} --- open-source projects in the genomic data ecosystem with contributors and users across multiple research institutions

\item Regularly translated complex distributed systems designs into plain language for non-technical researchers and presented this work publicly at \link{https://2025.pycascades.com/program/talks/data-science-garage/}{PyCascades 2025} and OHSU's Knight Science Day 2025 --- the customer-facing and stakeholder communication experience the role requires

\end{itemize}
