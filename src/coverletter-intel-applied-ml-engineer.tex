\paragraph{EXPERIENCE}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

I believe my experience maps directly to the Applied ML Engineer role at Intel --- in my previous positions I have:

\begin{itemize}

\item Designed and maintained end-to-end post-training pipelines using \link{https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth}{Unsloth} and \link{https://github.com/ollama/ollama}{Ollama}, covering data ingestion, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and local model deployment for AI-assisted developer workflows and automation prototyping

\item Ran and benchmarked quantized open-weight models (Gemma 4, Qwen 3) on local hardware, developing practical intuition for the tradeoffs between model size, inference speed, and output quality that inform distillation and quantization decisions

\end{itemize}
