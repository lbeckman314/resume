\paragraph{EXPERIENCE}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

As a Research Software Engineer at OHSU Knight Cancer Institute, I have spent the past two years doing work that touches several of the domains central to this role:

\begin{itemize}

\item Maintained and contributed to multiple open-source libraries used in production genomic research pipelines --- \link{https://github.com/calypr/funnel}{Funnel}, \link{https://github.com/ohsu-comp-bio/py-tes}{py-tes}, \link{https://github.com/nextflow-io/nf-ga4gh}{nf-ga4gh}, and \link{https://github.com/anvilproject/drs_downloader}{drs\_downloader} --- including API design, versioning, documentation, and cross-community maintenance in collaboration with GA4GH working groups.

\item Co-authored \link{https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13059-024-03431-3}{SyntheVAEiser} (Genome Biology 2024), a variational autoencoder applied to gene expression data, which required working closely with PyTorch-based ML training pipelines and understanding how numeric precision affects model outcomes.

\end{itemize}

I am genuinely motivated to move closer to the hardware layer, and I would welcome any conversation about how my background might contribute to Intel's AI performance work --- even in a supporting or adjacent capacity.
