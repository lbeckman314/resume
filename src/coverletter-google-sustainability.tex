To Whom it May Concern,

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

Climate action at the scale Google operates requires the same thing that ecological and genomics research does: pipelines that are reliable, data that is trustworthy, and engineers who can translate between complex systems and the humans who depend on them. That intersection is where I have spent the last several years.

\paragraph{EXPERIENCE}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

As a Research Software Engineer at OHSU's Knight Cancer Institute, I built and maintained data infrastructure handling genomic datasets at scale across cloud and HPC environments:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Designed and led development of a workflow orchestration platform integrating the \link{https://www.ga4gh.org/task-execution-services/}{GA4GH Task Execution API}, \link{https://www.nextflow.io/}{Nextflow}, and \link{https://gen3.org/}{Gen3-Workflow} --- processing large-scale biological data across AWS, Azure, GCP, and OpenStack

    \item Built ETL pipelines and data integration tooling connecting disparate research platforms and cloud data stores, with automated validation and audit logging, including hands-on work with BigQuery and data warehouse technologies through the \link{https://calypr.org/}{CALYPR platform}

    \item Developed CI/CD systems, compliance workflows, and a real-time \link{https://calypr.org/status-monitor}{monitoring dashboard} to ensure data integrity and operational reliability

    \item Collaborated closely with non-technical researchers to design systems they could understand, trust, and actually use --- including public presentations at \link{https://2025.pycascades.com/program/talks/data-science-garage/}{PyCascades 2025} and OHSU Knight Science Day 2025

\end{itemize}

\paragraph{SUSTAINABILITY BACKGROUND}\

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

My interest in sustainability is not incidental --- it is where my scientific career began. As an undergraduate researcher at the University of Minnesota, I led a phylogenetic study of neotropical \emph{Cecropia} trees and their ant mutualists (NSF-funded, College of Biological Sciences), applying Bayesian genomic analysis to understand co-evolutionary relationships in tropical ecosystems. I also conducted field research at the \link{https://andrewsforest.oregonstate.edu/}{HJ Andrews Experimental Forest} in Oregon, one of the longest-running ecological research sites in the United States and home to the NSF-funded Long Term Ecological Research program. This work was published in \link{https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/rec.12130}{\emph{Restoration Ecology}} (2014).

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

That background gives me something most data engineers working on sustainability problems do not have: I understand what the data represents. Carbon accounting, biodiversity metrics, and energy efficiency figures are not abstract numbers to me --- they connect to real ecological systems I have studied firsthand.

\vspace*{\baselineskip}

I'm based in Portland, one of the listed locations for this role, and would welcome the opportunity to bring both sides of that background --- ecological domain knowledge and production-grade data engineering --- to Google's climate action goals. Please see my attached resume for further details.
